Help and support

How do I start tracking study time?

Open Focus and press start. When you stop the session, its time is saved to your study records.

Why are my reminders not showing?

Reminders are checked only while this app is open. For browser notifications, allow them in your browser site settings. The Notifications section shows the current status.

How do I back up my data?

Open Storage and data and choose Export data. To restore, choose Import data and pick the backup file.
